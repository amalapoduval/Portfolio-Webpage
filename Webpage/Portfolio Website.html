About Me

I'm a multidisciplinary artist currently pursuing an M.A. in Visual & Experience Design in Berlin, with a BSc in Fashion Design and worked as a content writer and on a startup's social media team before landing here. I'm curious about how things are imagined, built, and brought to life, and that curiosity is what pulled me toward design in the first place. To me design has never been just about function, it has to work, but it also has to feel like something, that belief shapes how I approach every project. My perspective is shaped as much by culture as it is by process art, fashion, literature, architecture and music all inform how I think about design, not as separate references but as different expressions, people trying to communicate what it's like to be alive, in whatever form makes sense to them. I bring that same lens into my work. I want to make things that solve something real and still make someone stop and look twice.
